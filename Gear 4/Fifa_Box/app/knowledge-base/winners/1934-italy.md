# 1934 FIFA World Cup - Italy

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** Italy
- **Champion:** Italy
- **Runner-up:** Czechoslovakia
- **Final result:** Italy 2-1 Czechoslovakia (a.e.t.)
- **Final venue:** Stadio Nazionale PNF, Rome
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** Oldřich Nejedlý (Czechoslovakia) - 5 goals

## Summary
Italy won their first title on home soil under manager Vittorio Pozzo, in a tournament that used a straight knockout format from the first round with no group stage.

## Final Match
Italy 2-1 Czechoslovakia (a.e.t.), played at Stadio Nazionale PNF in Rome. Italy were crowned World Champions, with Czechoslovakia finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 1934 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by Italy.
- Top scorer of the tournament: Oldřich Nejedlý (Czechoslovakia) - 5 goals.
- Italy lifted the trophy after defeating Czechoslovakia in the final.
