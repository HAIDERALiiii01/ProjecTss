# 2006 FIFA World Cup - Germany

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** Germany
- **Champion:** Italy
- **Runner-up:** France
- **Final result:** Italy 1-1 France (Italy won 5-3 on penalties)
- **Final venue:** Olympiastadion, Berlin
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** Miroslav Klose (Germany) - 5 goals

## Summary
Remembered above all for Zinedine Zidane's headbutt on Marco Materazzi in extra time of the final, for which Zidane was sent off in his last professional match. Italy won their fourth title on penalties.

## Final Match
Italy 1-1 France (Italy won 5-3 on penalties), played at Olympiastadion in Berlin. Italy were crowned World Champions, with France finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 2006 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by Germany.
- Top scorer of the tournament: Miroslav Klose (Germany) - 5 goals.
- Italy lifted the trophy after defeating France in the final.
