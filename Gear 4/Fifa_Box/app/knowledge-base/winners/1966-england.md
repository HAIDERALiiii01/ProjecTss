# 1966 FIFA World Cup - England

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** England
- **Champion:** England
- **Runner-up:** West Germany
- **Final result:** England 4-2 West Germany (a.e.t.)
- **Final venue:** Wembley Stadium, London
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** Eusébio (Portugal) - 9 goals

## Summary
Hosts England won their only World Cup title, with Geoff Hurst scoring a hat-trick in the final - still the only hat-trick ever scored in a World Cup final. The third of his goals, which crossed the line or not, remains one of football's most debated moments.

## Final Match
England 4-2 West Germany (a.e.t.), played at Wembley Stadium in London. England were crowned World Champions, with West Germany finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 1966 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by England.
- Top scorer of the tournament: Eusébio (Portugal) - 9 goals.
- England lifted the trophy after defeating West Germany in the final.
