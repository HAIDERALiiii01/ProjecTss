# 1986 FIFA World Cup - Mexico

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** Mexico
- **Champion:** Argentina
- **Runner-up:** West Germany
- **Final result:** Argentina 3-2 West Germany
- **Final venue:** Estadio Azteca, Mexico City
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** Gary Lineker (England) - 6 goals

## Summary
Diego Maradona's tournament, most famous for the quarter-final against England featuring both the 'Hand of God' goal and the 'Goal of the Century' in the same match. Argentina won their second title.

## Final Match
Argentina 3-2 West Germany, played at Estadio Azteca in Mexico City. Argentina were crowned World Champions, with West Germany finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 1986 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by Mexico.
- Top scorer of the tournament: Gary Lineker (England) - 6 goals.
- Argentina lifted the trophy after defeating West Germany in the final.
