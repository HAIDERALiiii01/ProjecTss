# 2014 FIFA World Cup - Brazil

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** Brazil
- **Champion:** Germany
- **Runner-up:** Argentina
- **Final result:** Germany 1-0 Argentina (a.e.t.)
- **Final venue:** Maracanã, Rio de Janeiro
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** James Rodríguez (Colombia) - 6 goals

## Summary
Remembered largely for the semi-final in which Germany beat hosts Brazil 7-1, one of the biggest shocks in World Cup history. Germany went on to beat Argentina in the final, with Mario Götze scoring the winner in extra time.

## Final Match
Germany 1-0 Argentina (a.e.t.), played at Maracanã in Rio de Janeiro. Germany were crowned World Champions, with Argentina finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 2014 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by Brazil.
- Top scorer of the tournament: James Rodríguez (Colombia) - 6 goals.
- Germany lifted the trophy after defeating Argentina in the final.
