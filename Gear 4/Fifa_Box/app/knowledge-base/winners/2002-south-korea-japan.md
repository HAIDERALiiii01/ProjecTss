# 2002 FIFA World Cup - South Korea/Japan

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** South Korea/Japan
- **Champion:** Brazil
- **Runner-up:** Germany
- **Final result:** Brazil 2-0 Germany
- **Final venue:** International Stadium Yokohama, Yokohama
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** Ronaldo (Brazil) - 8 goals

## Summary
The first World Cup held in Asia and the first co-hosted by two nations. Brazil won a record fifth title behind Ronaldo, who scored twice in the final after recovering from serious injuries that had derailed his career. Co-hosts South Korea reached the semi-finals, the best finish by an Asian nation.

## Final Match
Brazil 2-0 Germany, played at International Stadium Yokohama in Yokohama. Brazil were crowned World Champions, with Germany finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 2002 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by South Korea/Japan.
- Top scorer of the tournament: Ronaldo (Brazil) - 8 goals.
- Brazil lifted the trophy after defeating Germany in the final.
