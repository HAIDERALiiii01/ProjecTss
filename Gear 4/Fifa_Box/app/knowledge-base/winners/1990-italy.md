# 1990 FIFA World Cup - Italy

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** Italy
- **Champion:** West Germany
- **Runner-up:** Argentina
- **Final result:** West Germany 1-0 Argentina
- **Final venue:** Stadio Olimpico, Rome
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** Salvatore Schillaci (Italy) - 6 goals

## Summary
A famously defensive, low-scoring tournament decided by a controversial late penalty in the final. West Germany won their third title just months before German reunification. Schillaci, a late squad addition, became the tournament's breakout star for the host nation.

## Final Match
West Germany 1-0 Argentina, played at Stadio Olimpico in Rome. West Germany were crowned World Champions, with Argentina finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 1990 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by Italy.
- Top scorer of the tournament: Salvatore Schillaci (Italy) - 6 goals.
- West Germany lifted the trophy after defeating Argentina in the final.
