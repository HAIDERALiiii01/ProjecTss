# 1954 FIFA World Cup - Switzerland

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** Switzerland
- **Champion:** West Germany
- **Runner-up:** Hungary
- **Final result:** West Germany 3-2 Hungary
- **Final venue:** Wankdorf Stadium, Bern
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** Sándor Kocsis (Hungary) - 11 goals

## Summary
Hungary's 'Golden Team' entered the final unbeaten in 4 years and had thrashed West Germany 8-3 in the group stage, but West Germany won the rematch in the final, a result remembered in Germany as the 'Miracle of Bern'. This tournament also had the highest average goals-per-game in World Cup history.

## Final Match
West Germany 3-2 Hungary, played at Wankdorf Stadium in Bern. West Germany were crowned World Champions, with Hungary finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 1954 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by Switzerland.
- Top scorer of the tournament: Sándor Kocsis (Hungary) - 11 goals.
- West Germany lifted the trophy after defeating Hungary in the final.
