# 1958 FIFA World Cup - Sweden

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** Sweden
- **Champion:** Brazil
- **Runner-up:** Sweden
- **Final result:** Brazil 5-2 Sweden
- **Final venue:** Råsunda Stadium, Solna
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** Just Fontaine (France) - 13 goals

## Summary
Brazil won their first title, with a 17-year-old Pelé scoring twice in the final. Just Fontaine's 13 goals in this single tournament remains the all-time record for goals scored by one player at a single World Cup, and has never been seriously threatened since.

## Final Match
Brazil 5-2 Sweden, played at Råsunda Stadium in Solna. Brazil were crowned World Champions, with Sweden finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 1958 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by Sweden.
- Top scorer of the tournament: Just Fontaine (France) - 13 goals.
- Brazil lifted the trophy after defeating Sweden in the final.
