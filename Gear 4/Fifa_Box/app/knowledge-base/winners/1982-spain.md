# 1982 FIFA World Cup - Spain

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** Spain
- **Champion:** Italy
- **Runner-up:** West Germany
- **Final result:** Italy 3-1 West Germany
- **Final venue:** Santiago Bernabéu, Madrid
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** Paolo Rossi (Italy) - 6 goals

## Summary
Paolo Rossi is the only player to win the Golden Boot and Golden Ball in the same tournament, scoring a hat-trick against favorites Brazil in the second group stage on his way to leading Italy to the title. This was also the first World Cup expanded to 24 teams.

## Final Match
Italy 3-1 West Germany, played at Santiago Bernabéu in Madrid. Italy were crowned World Champions, with West Germany finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 1982 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by Spain.
- Top scorer of the tournament: Paolo Rossi (Italy) - 6 goals.
- Italy lifted the trophy after defeating West Germany in the final.
