# 2010 FIFA World Cup - South Africa

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** South Africa
- **Champion:** Spain
- **Runner-up:** Netherlands
- **Final result:** Spain 1-0 Netherlands (a.e.t.)
- **Final venue:** Soccer City, Johannesburg
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** Thomas Müller (Germany) - 5 goals

## Summary
The first World Cup held in Africa. Spain won their first title, built on the tiki-taka style that also carried them to consecutive European Championships. Andrés Iniesta scored the winning goal in extra time.

## Final Match
Spain 1-0 Netherlands (a.e.t.), played at Soccer City in Johannesburg. Spain were crowned World Champions, with Netherlands finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 2010 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by South Africa.
- Top scorer of the tournament: Thomas Müller (Germany) - 5 goals.
- Spain lifted the trophy after defeating Netherlands in the final.
