# 2018 FIFA World Cup - Russia

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** Russia
- **Champion:** France
- **Runner-up:** Croatia
- **Final result:** France 4-2 Croatia
- **Final venue:** Luzhniki Stadium, Moscow
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** Harry Kane (England) - 6 goals

## Summary
France won their second title behind a young Kylian Mbappé, who became only the second teenager (after Pelé) to score in a World Cup final. VAR (Video Assistant Referee) was used at a World Cup for the first time. Croatia reached their first ever final.

## Final Match
France 4-2 Croatia, played at Luzhniki Stadium in Moscow. France were crowned World Champions, with Croatia finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 2018 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by Russia.
- Top scorer of the tournament: Harry Kane (England) - 6 goals.
- France lifted the trophy after defeating Croatia in the final.
