# 1930 FIFA World Cup - Uruguay

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** Uruguay
- **Champion:** Uruguay
- **Runner-up:** Argentina
- **Final result:** Uruguay 4-2 Argentina
- **Final venue:** Estadio Centenario, Montevideo
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** Guillermo Stábile (Argentina) - 8 goals

## Summary
The inaugural FIFA World Cup. Uruguay, chosen as hosts to mark the centenary of their independence, beat rivals Argentina in the final. Stábile scored 8 goals despite Argentina losing the final, a tournament-best haul that stood as a record for over two decades.

## Final Match
Uruguay 4-2 Argentina, played at Estadio Centenario in Montevideo. Uruguay were crowned World Champions, with Argentina finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 1930 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by Uruguay.
- Top scorer of the tournament: Guillermo Stábile (Argentina) - 8 goals.
- Uruguay lifted the trophy after defeating Argentina in the final.
