# 1962 FIFA World Cup - Chile

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** Chile
- **Champion:** Brazil
- **Runner-up:** Czechoslovakia
- **Final result:** Brazil 3-1 Czechoslovakia
- **Final venue:** Estadio Nacional, Santiago
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** Six-way tie on 4 goals: Flórián Albert, Valentin Ivanov, Garrincha, Vavá, Dražan Jerković, Leonel Sánchez

## Summary
Brazil retained their title even after losing Pelé to injury early in the tournament, with Garrincha stepping up as the tournament's standout player. No official tiebreaker rules existed yet, so six players shared the Golden Boot.

## Final Match
Brazil 3-1 Czechoslovakia, played at Estadio Nacional in Santiago. Brazil were crowned World Champions, with Czechoslovakia finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 1962 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by Chile.

- Brazil lifted the trophy after defeating Czechoslovakia in the final.
