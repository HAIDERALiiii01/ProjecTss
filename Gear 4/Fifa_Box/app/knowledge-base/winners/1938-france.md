# 1938 FIFA World Cup - France

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** France
- **Champion:** Italy
- **Runner-up:** Hungary
- **Final result:** Italy 4-2 Hungary
- **Final venue:** Stade Olympique de Colombes, Paris
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** Leônidas (Brazil) - 7 goals

## Summary
Italy became the first team to retain the World Cup. Leônidas, known as the 'Black Diamond', top-scored for Brazil despite Brazil finishing third; he did not play the semi-final, a decision that arguably cost Brazil a final berth.

## Final Match
Italy 4-2 Hungary, played at Stade Olympique de Colombes in Paris. Italy were crowned World Champions, with Hungary finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 1938 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by France.
- Top scorer of the tournament: Leônidas (Brazil) - 7 goals.
- Italy lifted the trophy after defeating Hungary in the final.
