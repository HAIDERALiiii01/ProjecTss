# 1970 FIFA World Cup - Mexico

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** Mexico
- **Champion:** Brazil
- **Runner-up:** Italy
- **Final result:** Brazil 4-1 Italy
- **Final venue:** Estadio Azteca, Mexico City
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** Gerd Müller (West Germany) - 10 goals

## Summary
Brazil won their third title, becoming the first team to win three World Cups and permanently keeping the Jules Rimet Trophy. This Brazil side, featuring Pelé, Jairzinho, and Carlos Alberto, is widely regarded as one of the greatest teams in football history.

## Final Match
Brazil 4-1 Italy, played at Estadio Azteca in Mexico City. Brazil were crowned World Champions, with Italy finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 1970 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by Mexico.
- Top scorer of the tournament: Gerd Müller (West Germany) - 10 goals.
- Brazil lifted the trophy after defeating Italy in the final.
