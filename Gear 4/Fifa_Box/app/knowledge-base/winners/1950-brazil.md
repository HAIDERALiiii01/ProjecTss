# 1950 FIFA World Cup - Brazil

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** Brazil
- **Champion:** Uruguay
- **Runner-up:** Brazil
- **Final result:** Uruguay 2-1 Brazil (final round-robin match)
- **Final venue:** Estádio do Maracanã, Rio de Janeiro
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** Ademir (Brazil) - 9 goals

## Summary
There was no single-match final; the tournament winner was decided by a four-team final round-robin group, and the deciding match between Uruguay and hosts Brazil produced the result. Brazil only needed a draw but lost 2-1, a result now known as the Maracanazo, played in front of an enormous crowd at the Maracanã.

## Final Match
Uruguay 2-1 Brazil (final round-robin match), played at Estádio do Maracanã in Rio de Janeiro. Uruguay were crowned World Champions, with Brazil finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 1950 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by Brazil.
- Top scorer of the tournament: Ademir (Brazil) - 9 goals.
- Uruguay lifted the trophy after defeating Brazil in the final.
