# 1974 FIFA World Cup - West Germany

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** West Germany
- **Champion:** West Germany
- **Runner-up:** Netherlands
- **Final result:** West Germany 2-1 Netherlands
- **Final venue:** Olympiastadion, Munich
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** Grzegorz Lato (Poland) - 7 goals

## Summary
West Germany, led by Franz Beckenbauer, beat a Netherlands side built around Johan Cruyff's 'Total Football' philosophy. The Netherlands took the lead inside two minutes from a penalty before West Germany came back to win.

## Final Match
West Germany 2-1 Netherlands, played at Olympiastadion in Munich. West Germany were crowned World Champions, with Netherlands finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 1974 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by West Germany.
- Top scorer of the tournament: Grzegorz Lato (Poland) - 7 goals.
- West Germany lifted the trophy after defeating Netherlands in the final.
