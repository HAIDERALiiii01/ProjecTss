# 1998 FIFA World Cup - France

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** France
- **Champion:** France
- **Runner-up:** Brazil
- **Final result:** France 3-0 Brazil
- **Final venue:** Stade de France, Saint-Denis
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** Davor Šuker (Croatia) - 6 goals

## Summary
Hosts France won their first title, with Zinedine Zidane scoring twice in the final. Croatia, in their first World Cup as an independent nation, finished third and Šuker was top scorer. The tournament expanded to 32 teams for the first time.

## Final Match
France 3-0 Brazil, played at Stade de France in Saint-Denis. France were crowned World Champions, with Brazil finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 1998 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by France.
- Top scorer of the tournament: Davor Šuker (Croatia) - 6 goals.
- France lifted the trophy after defeating Brazil in the final.
