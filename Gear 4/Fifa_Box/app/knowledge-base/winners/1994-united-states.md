# 1994 FIFA World Cup - United States

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** United States
- **Champion:** Brazil
- **Runner-up:** Italy
- **Final result:** Brazil 0-0 Italy (Brazil won 3-2 on penalties)
- **Final venue:** Rose Bowl, Pasadena
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** Shared: Hristo Stoichkov (Bulgaria) and Oleg Salenko (Russia) - 6 goals each

## Summary
The first World Cup final decided by a penalty shootout. Roberto Baggio's decisive miss in the shootout is one of the tournament's defining images. Oleg Salenko scored 5 goals in a single match against Cameroon, a World Cup record for one player in one game.

## Final Match
Brazil 0-0 Italy (Brazil won 3-2 on penalties), played at Rose Bowl in Pasadena. Brazil were crowned World Champions, with Italy finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 1994 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by United States.
- Top scorer of the tournament: Shared: Hristo Stoichkov (Bulgaria) and Oleg Salenko (Russia) - 6 goals each.
- Brazil lifted the trophy after defeating Italy in the final.
