# 1978 FIFA World Cup - Argentina

## Overview
- **Host nation(s):** Argentina
- **Champion:** Argentina
- **Runner-up:** Netherlands
- **Final result:** Argentina 3-1 Netherlands (a.e.t.)
- **Final venue:** Estadio Monumental, Buenos Aires
- **Golden Boot (top scorer):** Mario Kempes (Argentina) - 6 goals

## Summary
Hosts Argentina won their first title under military dictatorship rule, a tournament that remains politically controversial. Kempes was both top scorer and the outstanding player, scoring twice in the final.

## Final Match
Argentina 3-1 Netherlands (a.e.t.), played at Estadio Monumental in Buenos Aires. Argentina were crowned World Champions, with Netherlands finishing as runners-up.

## Quick Facts
- This was the 1978 edition of the FIFA World Cup, hosted by Argentina.
- Top scorer of the tournament: Mario Kempes (Argentina) - 6 goals.
- Argentina lifted the trophy after defeating Netherlands in the final.
