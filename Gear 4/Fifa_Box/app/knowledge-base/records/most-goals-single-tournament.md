# Record: Most Goals in a Single World Cup Tournament

## The Record
**Just Fontaine (France) - 13 goals at the 1958 World Cup in Sweden**, scored across just six matches, including four goals in the third-place playoff against West Germany. Fontaine averaged 2.17 goals per game.

## Why It Has Never Been Broken
The record has stood for over 65 years. The closest modern challengers, Ronaldo (8 goals, 2002) and Kylian Mbappé (8 goals, 2022), remain five goals short despite playing in eras with more matches available to finalists and, in Mbappé's case, more matches overall due to tournament expansion. Most observers consider it one of the most unbreakable records in football.

## Second Place
Sándor Kocsis (Hungary) scored 11 goals at the 1954 World Cup, the second-highest single-tournament total in history.
