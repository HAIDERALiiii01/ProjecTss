# Record: Fastest Goal in World Cup History

## The Record
**Hakan Şükür (Turkey)** scored the fastest goal in World Cup history, netting after just 11 seconds against South Korea in the third-place playoff at the 2002 World Cup.

## Context
The goal remains a widely cited World Cup trivia record and has not been threatened since. It came in a third-place match rather than a group or knockout stage fixture, which is a common pattern for fast, high-tempo World Cup records given lower defensive caution in dead-rubber matches.
