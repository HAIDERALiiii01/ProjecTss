# Record: Most Goals By One Player in a Single Match

## The Record
**Oleg Salenko (Russia) - 5 goals**, scored against Cameroon at the 1994 World Cup in the United States, a 6-1 win. This remains the only time a player has scored five goals in a single World Cup match.

## Context
Salenko shared the tournament's Golden Boot that year with Bulgaria's Hristo Stoichkov despite this standout individual performance, since both finished with 6 goals overall for the tournament (Salenko's other goal came in a different match).
