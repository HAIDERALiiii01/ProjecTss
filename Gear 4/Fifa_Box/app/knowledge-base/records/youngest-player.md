# Record: Youngest Player in World Cup History

## The Record
**Norman Whiteside (Northern Ireland)** became the youngest player to appear in a World Cup match at 17 years and 41 days old, at the 1982 World Cup in Spain.

## Youngest Goalscorer
**Pelé (Brazil)** remains the youngest player ever to score in a World Cup, doing so at 17 years old during the 1958 tournament. He is also the youngest player to score in a World Cup final, also in 1958.

## Context
Both records have stood for multiple decades and are frequently cited when discussing wonderkid or breakout performances at subsequent tournaments.
