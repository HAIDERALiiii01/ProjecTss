# Record: Host Nations That Won Their Own World Cup

## The Full List
Six nations have won the World Cup while hosting: Uruguay (1930), Italy (1934), England (1966), West Germany (1974), Argentina (1978), and France (1998).

## Context
No host nation has won the World Cup since France in 1998, a gap of over two decades through the 2022 tournament, despite hosts historically enjoying advantages such as local support, no travel requirements, and, in earlier tournaments, more favorable scheduling and officiating conditions than are typical in the modern era.

## 2026 Context
The 2026 World Cup, currently in progress as of this writing, is co-hosted by the United States, Canada, and Mexico. Whether the streak of no host nation winning since 1998 continues will be determined when the tournament concludes on July 19, 2026.
