# Record: Most Clean Sheets by a Goalkeeper (World Cup Career)

## Leading Goalkeepers
Goalkeepers who have played across multiple tournaments for historically strong defensive teams, such as Italy's Gianluigi Buffon and Germany's Manuel Neuer, rank among the career clean-sheet leaders, reflecting both individual quality and the defensive strength of the national teams they played behind.

## Context
Unlike single-tournament records, career clean-sheet totals depend heavily on how many tournaments a goalkeeper starts in and how far their team advances (more knockout matches means more opportunities for additional clean sheets against generally weaker attacking opposition in some rounds).
