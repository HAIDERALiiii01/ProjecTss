# Record: Most World Cup Titles Won By a Country

## The Record (through 2022)
**Brazil - 5 titles** (1958, 1962, 1970, 1994, 2002), the most of any nation.

## Full Top of the List
1. Brazil - 5
2. Germany/West Germany - 4
3. Italy - 4
4. Argentina - 3
5. France, Uruguay - 2 each

## Related Facts
Brazil is the only country to have played in every World Cup since the tournament began in 1930, and the only team to have won a World Cup on every continent that has hosted the tournament at the time of their wins.
