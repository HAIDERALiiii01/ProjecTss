# Records: Penalty Shootouts at the World Cup

## Key Facts
- The first World Cup final decided by a penalty shootout was 1994, when Brazil beat Italy 3-2 on penalties after a 0-0 draw, with Roberto Baggio's decisive miss becoming one of the tournament's most enduring images.
- The 2022 final was also decided on penalties, with Argentina beating France 4-2 after a 3-3 draw, following Kylian Mbappé's hat-trick.
- Goals scored during penalty shootouts do not count toward a player's official World Cup goal tally (they are recorded separately from open-play and extra-time goals).

## Notable Shootout Performances
Argentina goalkeeper Emiliano Martínez became a standout shootout performer at the 2022 World Cup, saving key penalties in both the round-of-16 win over the Netherlands and the final against France.
