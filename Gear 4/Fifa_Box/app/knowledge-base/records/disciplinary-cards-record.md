# Record: Most Cards in a Single World Cup Match

## The Record
The 2006 World Cup group-stage match between Portugal and Netherlands produced 16 yellow cards and 4 red cards, the most heavily officiated match in World Cup history, sometimes referred to as the "Battle of Nuremberg."

## Context
The match's card count remains an outlier even compared to other physical, high-stakes World Cup matches, and it is frequently cited in discussions of referee control and player discipline at the tournament.
