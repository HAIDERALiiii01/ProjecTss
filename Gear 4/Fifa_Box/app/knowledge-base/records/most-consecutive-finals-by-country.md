# Record: Most Consecutive World Cup Final Appearances

## Notable Streaks
Brazil, Germany, and Argentina have all had multi-tournament stretches of consistent deep runs, but no nation has appeared in more than two World Cup finals in a row in the modern 32-team era (since 1998). Brazil reached back-to-back finals in 1994 and 1998 (winning the first, losing the second). Germany reached three finals across four tournaments between 2002 and 2014 (2002, 2014, and appearing as runner-up/champion across that stretch), though not in three consecutive editions.

## Context
Reaching even two consecutive World Cup finals is a rare achievement given the four-year gap between tournaments, squad turnover, and the depth of competition, making any back-to-back final appearance a notable statistical outlier worth highlighting to users asking about sustained international dominance.
