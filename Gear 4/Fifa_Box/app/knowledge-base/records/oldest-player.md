# Record: Oldest Player in World Cup History

## The Record
**Essam El-Hadary (Egypt)**, a goalkeeper, became the oldest player to appear in a World Cup match at 45 years old, playing for Egypt at the 2018 World Cup in Russia. He also became the oldest player ever to save a penalty in a World Cup match during that tournament.

## Context
Goalkeepers, whose position relies less on pace and physical speed than outfield positions, disproportionately hold "oldest player" records across football generally, and El-Hadary's record fits that broader pattern.
