# Record: Most World Cup Goals (Career Total)

## The Record (through 2022)
**Miroslav Klose (Germany) - 16 goals** across four tournaments (2002, 2006, 2010, 2014), the all-time career scoring record.

## Chasers
Ronaldo Nazário (Brazil) sits second with 15 goals across three tournaments. Lionel Messi reached 13 goals through 2022 across five tournaments and is actively adding to his total at the 2026 World Cup, which is in progress as of this writing - his final 2026 tally should be checked to see whether he has surpassed Klose's record.

## Why This Differs From the Golden Boot
This is a cumulative, multi-tournament career record, distinct from the Golden Boot, which only rewards the top scorer within a single edition. Klose never won an outright Golden Boot (he shared leading-scorer status in some tournaments) but accumulated the highest career total through consistency across four World Cups.
