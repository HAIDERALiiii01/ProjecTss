# Record: Most World Cup Matches Played By an Individual

## The Record
**Lothar Matthäus (Germany) - 25 matches** across five tournaments (1982, 1986, 1990, 1994, 1998).

## Related Record: Most Tournaments Played
A small group of players have appeared in five separate World Cup tournaments, including Matthäus, Lionel Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo, Rafael Márquez (Mexico), and Antonio Carbajal (Mexico, the first player ever to do so, spanning 1950-1966). This requires remaining at elite international level for at least 16 years.
