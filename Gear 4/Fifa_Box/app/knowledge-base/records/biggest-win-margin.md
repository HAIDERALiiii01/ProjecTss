# Record: Biggest Winning Margin in a World Cup Match

## The Record
**Hungary 10-1 El Salvador**, at the 1982 World Cup in Spain, a 9-goal margin that remains the largest in tournament history.

## Other Notable Blowouts
- Yugoslavia 9-0 Zaire (1974)
- Germany 8-0 Saudi Arabia (2002)
- Hungary 9-0 South Korea (1954)

## Context
Most of the largest wins in World Cup history occurred in group-stage matches against nations making their World Cup debut or playing significantly outside their level at the time, reflecting an earlier era of the tournament with less parity between qualifying confederations than exists today.
