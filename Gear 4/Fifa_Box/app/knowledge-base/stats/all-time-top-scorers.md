# FIFA World Cup All-Time Top Scorers

## Overview
This file ranks the leading individual goalscorers across all FIFA World Cup tournaments combined (career totals, not single-tournament totals). Figures below are complete through the 2022 Qatar World Cup.

## All-Time Leaderboard (through 2022)
1. **Miroslav Klose (Germany)** - 16 goals across four tournaments (2002, 2006, 2010, 2014). Klose is the all-time record holder and the only player to reach 16 World Cup goals prior to 2026.
2. **Ronaldo Nazário (Brazil)** - 15 goals across three tournaments (1998, 2002, 2006).
3. **Gerd Müller (West Germany)** - 14 goals across two tournaments (1970, 1974).
4. **Just Fontaine (France)** - 13 goals, all scored in a single tournament (1958) - see the Records file for his single-tournament record.
5. **Lionel Messi (Argentina)** - 13 goals through 2022, across five tournaments (2006-2022). Messi is the only player to have appeared in five different World Cups scoring in each of them at that time.
6. **Pelé (Brazil)** - 12 goals across four tournaments (1958-1970).

## Important Note on 2026
Lionel Messi is currently playing at the 2026 World Cup and adding to his career tally in real time. Depending on how many goals he scores in 2026, he may surpass Klose's all-time record of 16. This file should be refreshed after the 2026 tournament concludes on July 19, 2026, to reflect final totals.

## Why Career Totals Are Different From Single-Tournament Totals
A player's all-time total is the sum of goals across every World Cup they have played in, while the Golden Boot recognizes only the top scorer within one tournament. Klose leads all-time despite never winning an outright Golden Boot in a single edition, because his scoring was spread consistently across four tournaments.
