# FIFA World Cup Titles By Country (Through 2022)

## Full Ranking
1. **Brazil** - 5 titles (1958, 1962, 1970, 1994, 2002)
2. **Germany / West Germany** - 4 titles (1954, 1974, 1990, 2014)
3. **Italy** - 4 titles (1934, 1938, 1982, 2006)
4. **Argentina** - 3 titles (1978, 1986, 2022)
5. **France** - 2 titles (1998, 2018)
6. **Uruguay** - 2 titles (1930, 1950)
7. **England** - 1 title (1966)
8. **Spain** - 1 title (2010)

## Key Facts
- Brazil is the only nation to have played in every single World Cup tournament since 1930.
- Brazil is the only team to win the World Cup on a continent other than its own (in Sweden 1958 and Japan/South Korea 2002, among others), while several European nations have historically struggled to win World Cups held in the Americas and vice versa (sometimes called the "European curse" in the Americas, though Germany broke this pattern in Brazil 2014).
- Argentina's third title in 2022 (Qatar) came 36 years after their second title in 1986.
- As of the 2022 tournament, no nation from Africa, Asia, or North America had ever won a World Cup title.

## Current Titleholder
Argentina is the reigning World Cup champion, having won the 2022 tournament in Qatar. The 2026 tournament, currently in progress as of this writing, will determine the next titleholder.
