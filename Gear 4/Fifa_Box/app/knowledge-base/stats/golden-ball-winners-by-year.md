# FIFA World Cup Golden Ball Winners (Best Player Award)

## Overview
The Golden Ball is awarded to the best overall player of the tournament, as voted by media, and is distinct from the Golden Boot (which rewards only goals scored). It was introduced in 1982.

## Key Facts
- Introduced at the 1982 World Cup in Spain, sponsored by Adidas and France Football.
- **Paolo Rossi (Italy, 1982)** is the only player to win both the Golden Ball and the Golden Boot in the same tournament.
- **Oliver Kahn (Germany, 2002)** is the only goalkeeper ever to win the Golden Ball.
- **Lionel Messi** won the Golden Ball at both the 2014 World Cup (as runner-up) and the 2022 World Cup (as champion), making him the first player in history to win it twice.
- At the 2022 World Cup, Messi won the Golden Ball with 7 goals while Kylian Mbappé won the Golden Boot with 8 goals, illustrating how the two awards can go to different players based on differing criteria (overall influence vs. pure goal count).

## Golden Ball vs Golden Boot - Why They Differ
The Golden Boot is a purely statistical award based on goals scored (with assists and minutes as tiebreakers). The Golden Ball is a subjective award voted on by a panel of media members, judging overall performance, influence, leadership, and importance to the team across the whole tournament - a player can win one without the other, as Messi and Mbappé did in 2022.

## Notable Winners Who Were Also Champions
Only three Golden Ball winners have also been part of the winning team in the same tournament: Pelé (1958), Kylian Mbappé (2018 - note: Mbappé won the 2018 Golden Ball category for best young player, not senior Golden Ball; the Golden Ball itself in 2018 went to Luka Modrić of runner-up Croatia), and Enzo Fernández (2022, as part of the FIFA Young Player Award alongside Argentina's title win). Care should be taken not to confuse the main Golden Ball, the Best Young Player award, and the Golden Boot, as they are three separate honors.
