# Hat-Tricks in FIFA World Cup History

## Overview
A hat-trick (three goals by one player in a single match) is a relatively rare feat at the World Cup given the tournament's short group-and-knockout format and the quality of opposition defenses.

## Notable Hat-Tricks
- **Geoff Hurst (England, 1966 final)** - The only hat-trick ever scored in a World Cup final, in England's 4-2 win over West Germany at Wembley. The legitimacy of his second goal (whether the ball crossed the line) remains debated to this day.
- **Kylian Mbappé (France, 2022 final)** - The second-ever hat-trick in a World Cup final, scored against Argentina in the 3-3 draw that Argentina won on penalties. Despite the hat-trick, Mbappé's team lost the match.
- **Oleg Salenko (Russia, 1994)** - Scored 5 goals in a single match against Cameroon, the record for most goals by one player in a single World Cup match.
- **Paolo Rossi (Italy, 1982)** - Scored a hat-trick against a heavily favored Brazil side in the second group stage, a result now considered one of the great upsets in World Cup history and a turning point in Italy's title run.
- Just Fontaine's 13-goal haul in 1958 included multiple multi-goal matches, including four goals in the third-place playoff against West Germany.

## Frequency
Hat-tricks have become somewhat more common in the post-2018 era due in part to VAR awarding penalties more consistently, though they remain a rare, tournament-defining achievement.
