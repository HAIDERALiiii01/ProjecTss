# FIFA World Cup Golden Boot Winners By Year (1930-2022)

## Overview
The Golden Boot (called the Golden Shoe before 2010) is awarded to the top individual goalscorer of each FIFA World Cup tournament. FIFA recognizes top scorers retroactively for every tournament back to 1930, even though the award was not formally created until 1982.

## Full List
- 1930 Uruguay: Guillermo Stábile (Argentina) - 8 goals
- 1934 Italy: Oldřich Nejedlý (Czechoslovakia) - 5 goals
- 1938 France: Leônidas (Brazil) - 7 goals
- 1950 Brazil: Ademir (Brazil) - 9 goals
- 1954 Switzerland: Sándor Kocsis (Hungary) - 11 goals
- 1958 Sweden: Just Fontaine (France) - 13 goals (all-time record for one tournament)
- 1962 Chile: Six-way tie at 4 goals each (Albert, Ivanov, Garrincha, Vavá, Jerković, Sánchez)
- 1966 England: Eusébio (Portugal) - 9 goals
- 1970 Mexico: Gerd Müller (West Germany) - 10 goals
- 1974 West Germany: Grzegorz Lato (Poland) - 7 goals
- 1978 Argentina: Mario Kempes (Argentina) - 6 goals
- 1982 Spain: Paolo Rossi (Italy) - 6 goals
- 1986 Mexico: Gary Lineker (England) - 6 goals
- 1990 Italy: Salvatore Schillaci (Italy) - 6 goals
- 1994 USA: Shared - Hristo Stoichkov (Bulgaria) & Oleg Salenko (Russia) - 6 goals each
- 1998 France: Davor Šuker (Croatia) - 6 goals
- 2002 Korea/Japan: Ronaldo (Brazil) - 8 goals
- 2006 Germany: Miroslav Klose (Germany) - 5 goals
- 2010 South Africa: Thomas Müller (Germany) - 5 goals
- 2014 Brazil: James Rodríguez (Colombia) - 6 goals
- 2018 Russia: Harry Kane (England) - 6 goals
- 2022 Qatar: Kylian Mbappé (France) - 8 goals

## Key Rules
- Since 1994, tiebreakers apply in order: most assists, then fewest minutes played per goal.
- Goals scored in penalty shootouts do NOT count toward Golden Boot totals; only goals scored in normal play, extra time, and in-game penalties count.
- No player has ever won the Golden Boot outright in two different tournaments. Several players have come close by finishing as a top scorer across multiple editions (e.g., Miroslav Klose was among the leading scorers across four consecutive tournaments from 2002-2014), but no repeat outright winner exists through 2022.

## 2026 Note
Kylian Mbappé is the defending Golden Boot holder heading into the 2026 tournament, which is in progress as of this writing. This file should be updated with the 2026 winner once the tournament concludes.
