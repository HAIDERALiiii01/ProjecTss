# Most FIFA World Cup Appearances (Matches Played)

## Overview
This file covers the players who have featured in the most individual matches across FIFA World Cup tournaments, a record distinct from goals scored.

## Leaders (through 2022)
- **Lothar Matthäus (Germany)** - 25 matches across five tournaments (1982-1998), the record for most World Cup matches played by any individual for decades.
- Several modern players have approached this mark. Cristiano Ronaldo, Lionel Messi, and other long-serving stars who have appeared across five World Cups (spanning roughly 16-20 years of a career) sit close to the top of this list.

## Most Tournaments Played (Distinct Editions)
Appearing in five separate World Cup tournaments is itself a rare feat requiring a player to remain at the top level of international football for at least 16 years (World Cups are held every four years). Players who have reached five World Cup tournaments include Lionel Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo, Lothar Matthäus, Rafael Márquez, and Antonio Carbajal.

## Why This Record Matters for a Chatbot
Questions like "who has played the most World Cup games" or "who has been to the most World Cups" are common trivia queries and refer to two related but distinct stats: total matches played (Matthäus's record) versus total distinct tournaments/editions attended (a smaller, more exclusive group of five-time World Cup players).
