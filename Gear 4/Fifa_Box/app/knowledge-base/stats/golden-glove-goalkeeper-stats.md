# FIFA World Cup Golden Glove Winners (Best Goalkeeper)

## Overview
The Golden Glove (originally the Lev Yashin Award, introduced in 1994) is awarded to the best goalkeeper of the tournament, as judged by FIFA's Technical Study Group.

## Winners (1994-2022)
- 1994: Michel Preud'homme (Belgium)
- 1998: Fabien Barthez (France)
- 2002: Oliver Kahn (Germany) - also won the Golden Ball the same year, the only goalkeeper to do so
- 2006: Gianluigi Buffon (Italy)
- 2010: Iker Casillas (Spain)
- 2014: Manuel Neuer (Germany)
- 2018: Thibaut Courtois (Belgium)
- 2022: Emiliano Martínez (Argentina) - saved a penalty in the final's shootout win over France, and was also decisive in Argentina's round-of-16 penalty shootout win over the Netherlands.

## Notable Goalkeeper Facts
- Oliver Kahn remains the only goalkeeper to win the World Cup's overall best player award (Golden Ball), doing so in 2002 despite Germany losing the final to Brazil.
- Goalkeepers are eligible for both the Golden Ball and Golden Glove in the same tournament, though this has only happened once (Kahn, 2002).
