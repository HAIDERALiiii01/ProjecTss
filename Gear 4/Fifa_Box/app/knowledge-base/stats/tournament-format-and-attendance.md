# FIFA World Cup Format Changes and Tournament Growth

## Team Count Over Time
- 1930-1934: 13-16 teams
- 1938-1978: 16 teams (with slight variation)
- 1982-1994: 24 teams
- 1998-2022: 32 teams
- 2026: 48 teams (largest expansion in tournament history)

## Match Count Growth
- The 32-team format (1998-2022) featured 64 matches per tournament.
- The 2026 tournament expands to 104 matches across 16 stadiums in 16 host cities across three countries.

## Group Stage Structure
- The 32-team era used 8 groups of 4 teams, with the top 2 from each group advancing to a 16-team knockout bracket (Round of 16).
- The 2026 format uses 12 groups of 4 teams. The top 2 from each group, plus the 8 best third-placed teams, advance to a newly introduced Round of 32.

## Other Notable Format Changes
- **1950**: Used a final round-robin group instead of a single final match.
- **1982**: First 24-team tournament, introducing a second group stage before the knockout rounds.
- **1994**: First tournament decided by a penalty shootout in the final (Brazil beat Italy).
- **2018**: First tournament to use VAR (Video Assistant Referee) technology.
- **2026**: First tournament to feature 48 teams and the first co-hosted by three nations.

## Why Format Matters for Stats Comparisons
Because the number of teams and matches has grown substantially over time (16 teams and ~18 matches in the early decades versus 48 teams and 104 matches from 2026), raw counting stats (most goals, most wins, etc.) from earlier tournaments are not always directly comparable to modern tournaments, which offer players and teams more matches in which to accumulate statistics.
