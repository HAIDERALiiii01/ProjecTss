# FIFA World Cup Host Nations By Year

## Full List of Hosts (1930-2026)
- 1930: Uruguay
- 1934: Italy
- 1938: France
- 1942 & 1946: Not held (World War II)
- 1950: Brazil
- 1954: Switzerland
- 1958: Sweden
- 1962: Chile
- 1966: England
- 1970: Mexico
- 1974: West Germany
- 1978: Argentina
- 1982: Spain
- 1986: Mexico (second time hosting)
- 1990: Italy (second time hosting)
- 1994: United States
- 1998: France (second time hosting)
- 2002: South Korea & Japan (first tournament co-hosted by two nations)
- 2006: Germany
- 2010: South Africa (first tournament held in Africa)
- 2014: Brazil (second time hosting)
- 2018: Russia
- 2022: Qatar (first tournament held in the Middle East, and the first played in November-December)
- 2026: United States, Canada & Mexico (first tournament co-hosted by three nations; Mexico's third time hosting)

## Hosts Who Won Their Own Tournament
Six nations have won the World Cup on home soil: Uruguay (1930), Italy (1934), England (1966), West Germany (1974), Argentina (1978), and France (1998). No host nation has won the tournament since France in 1998.

## Nations That Have Hosted Multiple Times
- Mexico: 1970, 1986, 2026 (three times, the most of any nation)
- Italy: 1934, 1990
- France: 1938, 1998
- Brazil: 1950, 2014
