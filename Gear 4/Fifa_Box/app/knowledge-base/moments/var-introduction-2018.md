# VAR's World Cup Debut (2018)

## What Happened
The 2018 World Cup in Russia was the first to use the Video Assistant Referee (VAR) system, allowing referees to review certain decisions (goals, penalties, red cards, and cases of mistaken identity) using video replay.

## Why It Matters
VAR led to a record number of penalties awarded at a single World Cup and was credited with correcting several major refereeing errors, though it also drew criticism for slowing down matches and for inconsistent application in its debut tournament.

## Legacy
VAR has been used at every World Cup since 2018 and has become a standard part of top-level football worldwide, fundamentally changing how contentious decisions like the 1966 "Hurst goal" or 1986 "Hand of God" would likely be adjudicated if they occurred today.
