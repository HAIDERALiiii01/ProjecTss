# Geoff Hurst's Final Hat-Trick (1966)

## What Happened
In the 1966 World Cup final at Wembley, England's Geoff Hurst scored three goals in the 4-2 extra-time win over West Germany, becoming the only player in history to score a hat-trick in a World Cup final.

## Why It Matters
His second goal, which gave England a 3-2 lead in extra time, remains one of the most debated moments in football history: the ball struck the underside of the crossbar and bounced down, with the referee's assistant ruling it had crossed the line. Goal-line technology did not exist at the time, and the decision remains disputed by German fans to this day.

## Legacy
England's only World Cup title to date came in this match, played on home soil. Hurst's achievement has never been repeated, though Kylian Mbappé came close by scoring a hat-trick in the losing side of the 2022 final.
