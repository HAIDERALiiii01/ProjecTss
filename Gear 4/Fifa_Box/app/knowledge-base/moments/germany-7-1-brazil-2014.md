# Germany 7-1 Brazil (2014)

## What Happened
In the semi-final of the 2014 World Cup, hosts Brazil were beaten 7-1 by Germany at the Mineirão stadium in Belo Horizonte, with Germany scoring five goals in a 29-minute span in the first half.

## Why It Matters
The result is one of the biggest shocks in World Cup history and the largest margin of defeat ever suffered by a host nation. It is often referred to in Brazil as the "Mineirazo," echoing the 1950 Maracanazo, and is considered one of the most humiliating results in Brazilian football history, compounded by the fact that star player Neymar was unavailable through injury and captain Thiago Silva was suspended.

## Legacy
Germany went on to win the tournament, beating Argentina in the final. The 7-1 scoreline remains a fixed reference point in discussions of football history and a painful memory for Brazilian fans.
