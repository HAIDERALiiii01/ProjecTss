# Mbappé's Hat-Trick in a Losing Final (2022)

## What Happened
In the 2022 World Cup final, Kylian Mbappé scored a hat-trick for France against Argentina, including two goals in the space of 97 seconds late in normal time to force extra time after Argentina had led 2-0. Despite his hat-trick, France lost the match 4-2 on penalties after a 3-3 draw.

## Why It Matters
Mbappé's hat-trick is only the second in World Cup final history (after Geoff Hurst in 1966) and the first by a player on the losing side. He also won the tournament's Golden Boot with 8 goals, matching Ronaldo's 2002 tally as the highest by an official Golden Boot winner.

## Legacy
The 2022 final is widely regarded as one of the greatest World Cup finals ever played, largely because of the drama created by Mbappé's comeback goals against a dominant Argentina side led by Lionel Messi.
