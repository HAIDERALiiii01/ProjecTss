# South Korea's Semi-Final Run (2002)

## What Happened
As co-hosts of the 2002 World Cup, South Korea beat Poland, Portugal, Italy, and Spain en route to becoming the first Asian nation to reach a World Cup semi-final, eventually losing to Germany.

## Why It Matters
The run included wins over major European football nations and was fueled by intense home support (the "Red Devils" fan movement). It remains the best World Cup performance by an Asian nation and a landmark moment for the growth of football across the continent.

## Legacy
The tournament significantly boosted football's popularity in South Korea and is often cited alongside Morocco's 2022 semi-final run as one of the two biggest results by a non-European, non-South-American nation in World Cup history.
