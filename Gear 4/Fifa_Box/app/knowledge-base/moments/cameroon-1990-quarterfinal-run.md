# Cameroon's Quarter-Final Run (1990)

## What Happened
Cameroon became the first African nation to reach the quarter-finals of a World Cup in 1990, beating defending champions Argentina in the opening match and eventually losing narrowly to England in extra time in the quarter-final.

## Why It Matters
38-year-old Roger Milla, coming out of retirement, became a global star during the tournament, known for his goal celebrations dancing at the corner flag. The run significantly raised the global profile of African football.

## Legacy
Cameroon's run stood as the best World Cup performance by an African nation for over three decades until Morocco reached the semi-finals in 2022.
