# Senegal Stuns Defending Champions France (2002)

## What Happened
In the opening match of the 2002 World Cup, Senegal, playing in their first-ever World Cup, beat defending champions France 1-0.

## Why It Matters
France entered the tournament as reigning World and European champions and one of the strongest sides in the world. The loss to a debutant nation remains one of the most notable opening-match shocks in World Cup history, and France went on to be eliminated in the group stage without scoring a single goal.

## Legacy
Senegal went on to reach the quarter-finals in that same tournament, the best World Cup finish by an African nation at the time (later matched by Morocco reaching the semi-finals in 2022).
