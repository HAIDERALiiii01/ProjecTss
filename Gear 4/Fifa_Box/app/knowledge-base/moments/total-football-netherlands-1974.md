# Netherlands' Total Football (1974)

## What Happened
The Netherlands reached the 1974 World Cup final playing "Total Football" (Totaalvoetbal), a fluid tactical system in which outfield players could interchange positions seamlessly, pioneered by club side Ajax and led on the international stage by Johan Cruyff.

## Why It Matters
Although the Netherlands lost the final 2-1 to West Germany, their style of play is considered one of the most influential tactical developments in football history, having a lasting impact on how the modern game is coached and played, particularly at Barcelona (via Cruyff's later role there) and subsequently across much of European football.

## Legacy
The 1974 Netherlands side is frequently cited as the greatest team never to win a World Cup, and Cruyff himself is remembered as one of the most influential figures in the sport's tactical history, on par with his status as a player.
