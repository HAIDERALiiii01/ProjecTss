# Iniesta's Extra-Time Winner (2010)

## What Happened
In the 116th minute of the 2010 World Cup final, with the score still 0-0, Andrés Iniesta scored the winning goal for Spain against the Netherlands, securing Spain's first-ever World Cup title.

## Why It Matters
The 2010 final was one of the most physical and card-heavy finals in World Cup history, with the Netherlands committing a series of hard fouls in an attempt to disrupt Spain's tiki-taka passing style. Iniesta's late strike broke a deadlocked, tense match.

## Legacy
The win completed a remarkable run for Spain, who had won the European Championship in 2008 and would go on to win it again in 2012, forming one of the most dominant international teams of the modern era. Iniesta later revealed he wore a shirt underneath his kit dedicated to a former teammate, Dani Jarque, who had died suddenly the previous year.
