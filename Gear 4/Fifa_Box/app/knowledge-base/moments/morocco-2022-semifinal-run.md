# Morocco's Historic Semi-Final Run (2022)

## What Happened
Morocco became the first African and first Arab nation to reach a World Cup semi-final at the 2022 tournament, beating Belgium, Spain, and Portugal along the way before losing to France in the semi-final and finishing fourth after losing the third-place match to Croatia.

## Why It Matters
Morocco eliminated three major European footballing nations (Belgium, Spain, and Portugal, the latter having eliminated Cristiano Ronaldo's side) en route to the semi-finals, a run unmatched by any African or Arab nation in World Cup history.

## Legacy
The run is considered a landmark achievement for African and Arab football, generating enormous celebrations across the Arab world and Africa and is frequently cited alongside South Korea's 2002 run as one of the great underdog stories of the modern World Cup era.
