# Pelé's World Cup Debut (1958)

## What Happened
A 17-year-old Pelé became the youngest player to appear in a World Cup at the time when he played for Brazil at the 1958 tournament in Sweden. He scored twice in the final as Brazil beat Sweden 5-2 to win their first title, becoming the youngest player to score in a World Cup final, a record that still stands.

## Why It Matters
Pelé's emergence in 1958 launched what became the greatest individual career in World Cup history, spanning four tournaments (1958, 1962, 1966, 1970) and three titles - a record for individual World Cup wins that remains unmatched.

## Legacy
Pelé's performance in the 1958 final is often cited as the moment Brazilian football announced itself as a global force, built on flair and individual skill that would come to define the country's footballing identity for decades.
