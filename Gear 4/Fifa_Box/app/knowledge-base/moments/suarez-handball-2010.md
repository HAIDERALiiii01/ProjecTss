# Suárez's Goal-Line Handball (2010)

## What Happened
In the closing seconds of extra time in the 2010 quarter-final between Uruguay and Ghana, Uruguay's Luis Suárez deliberately blocked a goal-bound shot with his hands on the goal line. He was sent off, but the resulting penalty was missed by Ghana's Asamoah Gyan, and Uruguay went on to win the match on penalties.

## Why It Matters
Ghana had a chance to become the first African nation to reach a World Cup semi-final, and Suárez's handball, while against the rules, prevented what would have been a certain goal in the final seconds. Suárez openly celebrated after the penalty was missed, drawing significant criticism.

## Legacy
The incident remains one of the most controversial moments in World Cup history and is frequently cited in debates about goal-line technology and the fairness of penalizing deliberate handballs with only a card and a penalty rather than an automatic goal.
