# Messi Finally Wins the World Cup (2022)

## What Happened
Lionel Messi captained Argentina to the 2022 World Cup title, scoring in the final and converting his penalty in the shootout win over France. It was Messi's fifth World Cup and widely assumed to be his last as a leading player.

## Why It Matters
Messi had previously reached the final in 2014 (losing to Germany) and had long been compared unfavorably to Diego Maradona by critics who argued a World Cup title was needed to cement his legacy as the greatest player of all time. The 2022 win, combined with a standout individual tournament in which he also won the Golden Ball, closed that debate for most observers.

## Legacy
Messi became the first player to win the World Cup Golden Ball twice (2014 and 2022). His iconic image lifting the trophy while wearing a traditional Qatari bisht (cloak) presented to him on the pitch is one of the most widely shared sporting images of the decade.
