# Italy Fails to Qualify (2018)

## What Happened
Italy, four-time World Cup champions, failed to qualify for the 2018 World Cup after losing a playoff to Sweden, marking the first time Italy had missed the tournament since 1958.

## Why It Matters
The failure to qualify shocked the football world given Italy's status as one of the sport's traditional powerhouses. Italy went on to miss the 2022 World Cup as well, marking back-to-back tournament absences for the first time in the nation's history.

## Legacy
Italy's qualification struggles in this era are frequently cited as one of the more surprising storylines of recent World Cup cycles and prompted extensive debate in Italian football about the state of the national program.
