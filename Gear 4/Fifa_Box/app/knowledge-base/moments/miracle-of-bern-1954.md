# The Miracle of Bern (1954)

## What Happened
Hungary's "Golden Team," unbeaten for over four years and having thrashed West Germany 8-3 in the group stage of the same tournament, were overwhelming favorites entering the 1954 final. West Germany won the rematch 3-2, coming back from 2-0 down.

## Why It Matters
The result is remembered in Germany as the "Miracle of Bern" (Wunder von Bern) and is considered a pivotal moment in post-war West German national identity and recovery. Puma and Adidas both trace early marketing breakthroughs to this tournament, including screw-in studs that reportedly helped West Germany's players in wet conditions during the final.

## Legacy
Hungary's Golden Team, despite this loss, is still regarded as one of the greatest teams never to win a World Cup. The match has inspired multiple films and books in Germany and remains one of the most culturally significant matches in World Cup history.
