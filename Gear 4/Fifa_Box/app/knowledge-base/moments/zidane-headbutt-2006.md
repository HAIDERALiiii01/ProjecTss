# Zidane's Headbutt (2006)

## What Happened
In extra time of the 2006 World Cup final between Italy and France, French captain Zinedine Zidane headbutted Italian defender Marco Materazzi in the chest after an exchange of words, and was sent off. Italy went on to win the match on penalties.

## Why It Matters
The incident occurred in what was Zidane's final professional match before retirement, ending an iconic career on a controversial note. It was widely covered internationally, with intense speculation about what Materazzi said to provoke Zidane (Materazzi later confirmed he had insulted Zidane's sister).

## Legacy
The moment remains one of the most replayed and discussed images in World Cup history, and Zidane later stated he did not regret the reaction despite the circumstances of his exit from the sport.
