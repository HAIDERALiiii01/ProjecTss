# The Hand of God (1986)

## What Happened
In the quarter-final between Argentina and England at the 1986 World Cup, Diego Maradona opened the scoring by punching the ball into the net with his hand, unnoticed by the referee. Argentina won the match 2-1 and went on to win the tournament.

## Why It Matters
Maradona later described the goal as being scored "a little with the head of Maradona and a little with the hand of God," giving the moment its enduring name. It remains one of the most controversial goals in football history and a frequently cited case study for the eventual introduction of video review technology (VAR), which was not implemented until 2018.

## Legacy
The goal is inseparable from Argentine and English football history and is regularly referenced in discussions of sportsmanship, officiating, and the case for technology in football.
