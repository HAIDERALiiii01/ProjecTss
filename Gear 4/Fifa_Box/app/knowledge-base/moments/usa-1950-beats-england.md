# The Miracle on Grass: USA 1-0 England (1950)

## What Happened
At the 1950 World Cup in Brazil, a United States team made up largely of part-time amateur players beat England, one of the tournament favorites and considered among the best teams in the world at the time, 1-0.

## Why It Matters
The result is often called football's "Miracle on Grass" and remains one of the greatest upsets in the sport's history. The scoreline was considered so implausible in Britain that some newspapers initially assumed it was a misprint for a 10-1 England win.

## Legacy
The match is frequently referenced whenever a heavy underdog beats a football powerhouse and is a landmark moment in the otherwise slow historical development of the sport in the United States.
