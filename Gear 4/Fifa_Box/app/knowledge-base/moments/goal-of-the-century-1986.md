# The Goal of the Century (1986)

## What Happened
Four minutes after the "Hand of God" goal, in the same quarter-final against England, Diego Maradona collected the ball in his own half and dribbled past five England players over roughly 60 meters before slotting past the goalkeeper.

## Why It Matters
The goal was later voted FIFA's "Goal of the Century" in a global internet poll in 2002. It is widely considered the greatest individual goal in the history of the sport and stands in stark contrast to the controversial goal scored minutes earlier in the same match, making the England-Argentina 1986 quarter-final one of the most talked-about single matches in World Cup history.

## Legacy
The goal cemented Maradona's status as one of the greatest players of all time and is a centerpiece of Argentina's run to the 1986 title, Maradona's first World Cup win as captain.
