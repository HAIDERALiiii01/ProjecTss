# North Korea Stuns Italy (1966)

## What Happened
At the 1966 World Cup in England, North Korea, playing in their first-ever World Cup and largely unknown to the football world, beat Italy 1-0 in the group stage to reach the quarter-finals.

## Why It Matters
The result remains one of the biggest upsets in World Cup history given the gulf in football pedigree and experience between the two nations at the time. North Korea then led Portugal 3-0 in the quarter-final before eventually losing 5-3 to a Portugal side inspired by a Eusébio hat-trick.

## Legacy
The story of the 1966 North Korea team was the subject of the acclaimed 2002 documentary "The Game of Their Lives," and the result remains a touchstone reference for football "underdog" stories.
