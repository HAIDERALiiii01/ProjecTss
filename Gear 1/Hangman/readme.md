## 🎯 What it does

A terminal-based Hangman game with a twist — instead of guessing letters, you answer **pop culture trivia questions**. Get too many wrong and your friend pays the price. Questions cover anime, football, Marvel, and... life choices.

---

## 📁 Files

| File | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `hangman.py` | 🚀 The entire game — run this to play |

---

## ⚙️ How it works

1. **Setup** — The game introduces the stakes with dramatic flair and a countdown
2. **Questions** — 6 trivia questions are asked one by one with 4 options each
3. **Scoring** — You need to get at least 3 correct (max 3 wrong answers allowed)
4. **Verdict** — Your friend is either saved 🎉 or hanged ☠️ based on your answers

## 🎮 Gameplay

- 6 questions total
- 4 options per question (enter the number)
- **3 or fewer wrong** → your friend survives 🎉
- **More than 3 wrong** → your friend is gone ☠️

Topics include:
- Jujutsu Kaisen
- FIFA World Cup 2022
- Euro Cup 2024
- Marvel Cinematic Universe
- Real talk about your studies 👀

---

## 📝 Notes

- No external libraries required — pure Python
- Questions are hardcoded in `hangman.py` — feel free to swap them out
- The game uses `time.sleep()` for dramatic effect, so don't panic if it feels slow
