# My Story: Background, Learning Journey, and Motivation

## Who I am

I am Haider Ali, a BSCS student at Federal Urdu University in Karachi, Pakistan. I focus on artificial intelligence, machine learning, generative AI, LLMs, and agentic AI. My main career direction is AI/ML rather than general software development, and I am especially interested in LLM applications, RAG, AI agents, agentic systems, and practical AI engineering.

## How I got started

In my journey, I started learning programming toward the end of 2024 and began my university degree in early 2025. As I learned more about programming, I became increasingly interested in artificial intelligence and machine learning. Since then I have built my skills through a mix of university studies, structured online learning, experimentation, and personal projects. When university started, I kept learning outside the curriculum and put significant effort into building practical skills independently.

## My university studies

I am pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at Federal Urdu University in Karachi. The degree has given me foundations in data structures and algorithms, database management systems, discrete structures, computer organization and assembly, differential equations, technical and business writing, and theory of automata. I use coursework to strengthen my fundamentals while I focus independently on AI/ML technologies and practical development.

## How my learning moved toward AI/ML

My learning path moved gradually from general programming toward machine learning and then toward modern AI systems. I started with Python and programming fundamentals, then machine learning and deep learning, then PyTorch and other ML frameworks. After that came LLMs and generative AI, RAG and vector databases, fine-tuning and QLoRA, tool calling and LLM APIs, AI agents, and finally agentic AI and multi-agent systems. As my interests developed, I became more interested in building systems around models, not only training models.

## My LLM and generative AI journey

In my journey with LLMs, I started with modern LLM APIs and open-source models and experimented with different ways of building AI applications. I have worked with OpenAI models, Google Gemini, Llama models, and Hugging Face Transformers, and with RAG, embeddings, vector databases, reranking, fine-tuning, and tool calling. I have used both hosted frontier models and open-source models, choosing between them based on what each project needed.

## My focus on agentic AI

More recently, my focus has shifted toward agentic AI. I am interested in how several components can work together toward a goal: planning, using tools, retrieving information, making decisions, and taking actions. This led me to explore agent orchestration, planning agents, autonomous agents, tool-based agents, multi-agent systems, function calling, and LLM workflows with LangChain.

DealScout is my main agentic AI project. It combines LLMs, RAG, planning, tool calling, and autonomous agents in one system, and it represents my shift from building individual AI features to building systems where several AI components work together.

## How I learn: by building projects

I learn primarily by building. Instead of only following tutorials, I turn what I learn into working projects, and mine cover RAG, LLM applications, fine-tuning, computer vision, image generation, AI agents, data processing, web scraping, and APIs. Some began as experiments while I was learning a technology, and others were built as larger portfolio projects.

When I learn a new technology, I try to understand what problem it solves, why it exists, how it works, how its parts interact, when to use it, and how to implement it in a real project. I don't want to memorize APIs or copy implementations. I try to understand the reasoning behind the code and architecture.

## My current direction and goal

My current focus is becoming an AI/ML engineer with a strong practical foundation. I am concentrating on LLM applications, RAG, agentic AI, LLM orchestration, tool calling, fine-tuning, and computer vision, and I keep building projects while I look for opportunities. My goal is to start my professional career through an internship, part-time role, trainee position, or junior-level opportunity, working on real AI systems and learning from experienced engineers.

## What drives my learning

My main motivation is becoming capable of building things independently. I value practical skills, experimentation, and continuous learning, and I want my projects to show what I can actually build, not to rely only on academic credentials. My long-term goal is to keep improving as an engineer and eventually build increasingly sophisticated AI systems.
